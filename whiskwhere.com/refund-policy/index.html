Refund Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

WhiskWhere is operated by Daniel McNeillie, doing business as reviv.

The short version: WhiskWhere is an app, not a store shelf. Nothing ships, so there’s nothing to return — and because every purchase is made through the Apple App Store or Google Play, refunds run through them. This page explains how, and what to do if something goes wrong. Email us anytime — a person answers. hello@whiskwhere.com

1. Nothing to return

WhiskWhere is software: a subscription (WhiskWhere Mise) and in-app request refills. There are no physical goods, no deliveries, and no returns. If you no longer want WhiskWhere, you can cancel your subscription — see Section 3 — and you can delete your account anytime in the app under Settings → Danger Zone.

2. How refunds work

Subscriptions and refills are purchased inside the app, and Apple or Google processes the payment. That means refund requests are made to, and decided by, the store where you bought:

  • Apple (iPhone and iPad): go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find your WhiskWhere purchase, and request a refund.
  • Google (Android): open Google Play, go to your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, find the WhiskWhere order, and request a refund.

Each store applies its own refund policies. We can’t issue refunds for store purchases ourselves — the stores don’t give us that ability — but Section 5 explains how we help when something’s gone wrong.

3. Cancelling is different from a refund

You can cancel Mise anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your plan stays active until the end of the period you’ve already paid for, and your banked and refill requests remain yours. Cancelling doesn’t, by itself, refund the current period — for that, use the store process in Section 2.

4. Request refills

A refill (300 requests for $4.99) is bought through the same stores, so refunds for refills follow the same process as subscriptions — Section 2. Unused refills never expire while your account is active.

5. If something went wrong, talk to us

Charged twice? Charged in error? A renewal you didn’t expect? The store turned you down and you believe that’s wrong? Email hello@whiskwhere.com and tell us what happened. Where a refund is in our power to give, we’ll make it right. Where it’s the store’s decision, we’ll help you put the request in front of them properly. And before disputing a charge with your bank, please email us first — most problems get fixed faster that way.

6. Promotions and gifts

Subscriptions received through a promotion or gift have no cash value and aren’t refundable.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app before the change takes effect.

8. Contact

Questions about refunds: hello@whiskwhere.com
