To delete your account through the app

  1. Navigate to the Settings page from the Home tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom. Look for the card entitled “Danger zone”. Click the “Delete my account” button.
  3. Type DELETE in the field provided and then click the “Delete forever” button.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account and every space, item, list, recipe, and photo you’ve saved. If you’re the only person in your kitchen, all of its data is deleted with your account. This cannot be undone.

One thing deleting can’t do: it does not cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google. If you have WhiskWhere Mise, cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings too, so you’re not billed for an account that no longer exists. See our Refund Policy.
