Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

WhiskWhere is operated by Daniel McNeillie, doing business as reviv (“we,” “us”). WhiskWhere is a kitchen organization app with an intelligent assistant that helps you keep track of your kitchen.

This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, how it’s used, who processes it for us, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email us — a person answers. hello@whiskwhere.com

1. What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and sign-in information (including sign-in through Apple or Google, if you use it), plus your household’s assistant name and preferences.
  • Your kitchen data. The items, spaces, quantities, and expiration dates you add; your recipes, meal plans, and shopping lists; and your food-spending entries. This is the content you create in the app — it exists so the app can work.
  • Kitchen activity. A record of activity in your kitchen — what was added, used, or saved — which powers features like your savings totals and weekly recap. This is the memory of the app.
  • Your conversations with your assistant. When you talk or type to your assistant, your conversations are stored with your kitchen — for your household only — so your assistant can remember what you’ve talked about and your conversation is there when you come back, switch kitchens, or move to a new phone. Your conversations are never shared with other households, never sold, and never used for advertising. Feedback you choose to send is covered separately — see Section 4.
  • Voice audio. If you talk to your assistant, your speech is converted to text so it can understand you; when it speaks back, text is converted to audio. See Section 3.
  • Photos you submit. If you photograph a shelf, receipt, or item for identification — or attach a screenshot to a feedback report — that image is processed for that purpose.
  • Subscription status. Whether you are on the free plan or a paid plan, and your request balance. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see or store your card number.
  • Feedback you choose to send. If you tell your assistant something is broken, confusing, or could be better, that report is saved so it can be reviewed and fixed. A report includes the conversation around it, and a screenshot if you choose to attach one. See Section 4.
  • Basic technical data. Device type, app version, and crash or error information needed to keep the app working.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: track your kitchen, answer your questions, plan meals, build shopping lists, and remember your kitchen for you.
  • To operate your subscription: count requests against your plan and apply rollovers and refills.
  • To fix and improve the app: feedback and error data help us find and repair problems.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising, and the app contains no advertising trackers. Your kitchen data is never pooled with other households’ data.

3. Who processes data for us

We use a small number of service providers, only to run features you use:

  • Meta Model API (Muse Spark). Your assistant’s replies are generated by Meta’s model API. When you talk to your assistant, your message and the relevant kitchen context are sent to the API so it can return a reply. WhiskWhere uses the API’s Standard data tier, which does not use request data to train models.
  • ElevenLabs. Converts your speech to text and/or your assistant’s replies to speech when you use voice features. Audio and text are processed only to perform that conversion.
  • Apple, Google, and RevenueCat. Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes your payment; RevenueCat helps us verify and manage your subscription status. Your payment details go to Apple or Google only — never to WhiskWhere.
  • ClickUp (feedback routing). If you send feedback, the report — including the conversation around it and any screenshot you attach — is delivered into our internal task system so it can be reviewed and acted on. This happens only when you actively send feedback.

Your kitchen data is stored in WhiskWhere’s own backend, under our control.

4. Feedback, specifically

  • Feedback is conversational and always started by you: you tell your assistant something is wrong or could be better, in your own words.
  • The report includes the conversation around the issue — and a screenshot, if you attach one — because that’s what makes the problem understandable and fixable.
  • When a report has been addressed, your assistant may let you know the next time you talk.
  • Nothing is sent unless you choose to report it, and the app tells you plainly at the point of reporting that the report may include the conversation, so there are no surprises.

5. Retention

  • Your account, kitchen data, and kitchen activity are kept while your account is active, so the app can remember your kitchen — that memory is the product.
  • Your conversations are kept while your account is active, as part of your kitchen’s memory. When you delete your account, your conversations are deleted with the rest of your data as described above.
  • When you delete your account (Section 6), your personal data is deleted within 30 days, except limited records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This removes your account and associated data as described in Section 5.
  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your kitchen data in the app at any time. For a copy of your data, or any other privacy request, email hello@whiskwhere.com and a person will handle it.
  • If you are in Canada, you may also contact us to access or correct your personal information, or to ask how it is handled.

7. Children

WhiskWhere is a household app for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Household members of all ages may be present while the app is used; account holders are responsible for their household’s use.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on access-controlled systems. No system is perfectly secure — but protecting your data is a founding commitment of this company, not an afterthought. We hold the app ourselves specifically so your kitchen data isn’t an asset on someone else’s shelf.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
