Lantern

It's something we all carry.

Child sex trafficking hides in plain sight — in games, in group chats, in the ordinary places where kids live. It starts quietly, with attention that looks like kindness. Most adults never see it, not because they don't care, but because nobody ever showed them what to look for.

Lantern puts a light in everyone's hand. It is a simple, trusted tool that helps parents, teachers, coaches, and church communities learn how grooming works, recognize the signs, report safely, and find help — before a child is lost in the dark.

Learn

How grooming and online recruitment actually work, in plain language — the slow trust-building, the gifts, the secrets, the red flags that look like kindness.

Recognize

Practice scenarios built from real patterns, so you learn to trust your gut faster when something feels wrong around a kid in your care.

Report safely

One tap to the people who can act — the National Human Trafficking Hotline and the NCMEC CyberTipline — with guidance on what helps. Never confront. Never try to rescue alone.

Find help

Vetted local resources and shelters by area, for victims and for the families standing next to them.

Not heroes. Stewards.

Lantern is not built for vigilantes. It is built for stewards — ordinary people who watch over the kids in their care and take that watch seriously. No public accusations. No confrontation. Just light, carried carefully, by people who refuse to look away.

It will be built the way everything at reviv is built: with established organizations in this fight reviewing the content, with a quick exit and a discreet presence on the phone, and with nothing stored that could endanger a child. Technology people can trust, in service of people who can't protect themselves.
