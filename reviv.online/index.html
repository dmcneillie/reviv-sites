Our first app

WhiskWhere

“Cook more, waste less, organize everything…
Finally a kitchen that remembers.”

WhiskWhere is that kitchen that remembers. Today it’s an app — and everything we know about your kitchen, you share with us. You’re the source; we’re the steward.

We hold what you share carefully. We never abuse it. And we hand it back in ways that can help: what needs using before it spoils, what’s for dinner tonight, and a plan for the week ahead — one that fits the time you actually have. Only have 35 minutes? Say so, and get an answer built for your evening. With your kitchen assistant, you don’t hunt through lists. You just talk like you’re talking to a person. And we can finally answer the question together: what’s for dinner?

Tomorrow it’s a tool that lives beyond the phone — on your speaker, in your kitchen, anywhere you ask. The app is where this starts.

Come on in, have a conversation about your kitchen,
and let’s see what we can cook together.
